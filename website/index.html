BEFORE YOU SETTLE IN

A few good questions.

Is Tern a browser or an extension?+

Tern is a standalone desktop browser with real Chromium pages. Tasks give your usual websites a place alongside your notes and next steps.

What happens when I put a task aside?+

The task moves to Later, with its pages and your saved note. Live pages stay running while Tern stays open. Putting a task aside does not save a form or submit anything to a website.

Will everything survive a restart?+

Your tasks, saved notes and page addresses survive a restart. Pages reopen from their addresses. Unsaved form edits and live website state do not survive quitting, so save your work on the website first.

Do I have to use AI?+

No. Browsing, tasks, notes and saved findings work with AI off. Optional assistance uses a local model. It uses task context such as notes, page titles and saved findings, without automatically reading page bodies or form fields. First use downloads the model.

Can I download Tern yet?+

Tern is a local Linux alpha. There isn't a public download yet. The interaction above is a small demonstration of putting a task aside and resuming it, not the full browser.
