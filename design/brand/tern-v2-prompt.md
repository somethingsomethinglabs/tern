## Initial prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Create a new logo concept for "tern", a desktop web browser that keeps unfinished tasks and lets people return to their work with context. This is a fresh second attempt at the identity.

Design a sophisticated, distinctive, compact abstract glyph that suggests a tern banking in flight and subtly a lowercase t. Two or three broad, tapered shapes with a deliberate open negative-space cut, long sharp wings, asymmetrical forward movement, optical balance. Abstract enough to read first as a memorable software mark. Aim for the restraint and precision of an excellent independent type foundry's identity. A light, agile, confident silhouette. No literal bird face, eye, feathers, heavy torso, spread-eagle pose, star-shaped tail, generic checkmark, globe, circular arrow, paper plane, or leaf. Avoid the Twitter bird silhouette.

Flat single-ink vector-style artwork, clean accurate edges, no gradients, shadows, bevels, texture, 3D or mockup photography. Forest green #245C4D and warm ivory #F6F7F2, with dark ink #22352F for type.

Deliver one beautifully composed landscape identity presentation on warm ivory. In the main upper area show the new green glyph at generous scale next to the lowercase wordmark "tern" in carefully spaced, distinctive humanist sans-serif lettering. Use ample whitespace. In a much smaller lower row show the SAME exact glyph reversed in ivory on a forest green rounded-square app tile, then the glyph alone at a small favicon-like size. Consistent geometry throughout. Only text is "tern", no headers, explanations, captions, tagline, colour codes or dimension labels. This is ONE resolved design, not a sheet of different concepts.
